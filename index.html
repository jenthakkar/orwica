    Questions

    Are Orwica products suitable for all hair and skin types?

    They are made for everyday use on all hair and skin types. If you have sensitive skin or a known allergy, do a patch test on your inner arm 24 hours before first use.

    How do I order?

    Tap Ask on WhatsApp on any product. Your message opens with the product name filled in. We confirm the price and delivery with you there.

    How often should I use the hair mask?

    Once a week. Mix with curd, apply to scalp and hair, leave for 1 to 2 hours and rinse well.

    Can the face pack be used as a face wash?

    Yes. It can be used as a gentle daily face wash, a face pack, a facial scrub or a body wash. Mix with water, rose water or curd depending on your skin.
